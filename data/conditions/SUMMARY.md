## Rules applied to every table

- A test condition is the state **before or at the start** of the test. Readings taken after the test ("XO2 after test", "XO2 (final)", "pfinal", "Calibrated final O2") are kept as outcomes (`outcome:o2_mole_fraction_after_test`, `outcome:pressure_after_test`), never as points on a condition axis: a final reading can follow suppression or extinction (BASS-II final O2 goes down to 0.6 %).
- Cells that say there is no single value (en dash, `RAMP`, `RAMPED`, `LOW O2`, `n/a`, spreadsheet errors) give no record and are listed in `extraction/skipped_cells.csv`.
- Unit conversions use exact factors (mmHg x 101.325/760, atm x 101.325, bar x 100, percent x 0.01, micron x 0.001, C + 273.15); a converted value longer than 6 significant digits is rounded to 6, and the record's note says so.

## Table readings that rely on a judgement

Each of these is applied by code, the same way to every row, and is also listed in [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md).

- **PSI-10**: Flame Design inverse-flame "XO2" is the O2 mole fraction of the burner stream (`burner_o2_mole_fraction`), not the ambient O2.
- **PSI-10**: PSI-10 has no license on its PSI page or record (checked 2026-11-14): every record carries `license not stated` in its note.
- **PSI-25**: BASS-II "Air display" has no unit in the table. The BASS-II ReadMe (`psi-data/PSI-25/Science Documents/PSI-25_Science Documents_BASS-II_ReadMe.docx`, paragraph 20) states: "Air display:  flow speed measured in the duct in cm/s on the video display.  Response time is very slow (~ 1 min), so reading is only steady for long times at the same flow setting." Cells that list only numbers become a `flow_velocity` range (lowest..highest reading during the test); cells mixing numbers and words are `insufficient_evidence`.
- **PSI-25**: BASS-II "Fan display" is not converted: the same ReadMe defines it as "instantaneous fan voltagex10", not a velocity.
- **PSI-25**: BASS-II `sample_thickness` is parsed from the material description ("100 micron", "1 mm thick", "0.2 mm PMMA film", "4 mm 1 sided"). Rod diameters (e.g. 1/4") and sample widths are not thicknesses and give no record. A description stating two different thicknesses is `insufficient_evidence`.
- **PSI-26**: BASS as-run matrix (`psi-data/PSI-26/Science Documents/PSI-26_Science Documents_As Run_Test Matrix.xlsx`, sheet "as run matrix"): the column "O2 % by volume" holds values such as 0.221 and 0.1707. They are read as mole fractions (22.1 %, 17.07 %), not as 0.221 %: a flame cannot burn at 0.2 % oxygen and the tests did burn. Both this column and "pressure, atm" sit under the header group "ISS environment"; the table does not say whether they were read in the station cabin or inside the glovebox work volume (values down to 0.159 suggest a diluted work volume, which is not stated).
- **PSI-26**: BASS rows whose material reads "SOOT Cleaning" are not tests and give no record.
- **PSI-39**: CFI "Oxygen", "Nitrogen" and "Helium" columns carry no unit. They are read as ambient mole fractions because the three sum to 1 in every row; `diluent` is N2, He or `mixed` from the two inert columns.
- **PSI-68**: FLEX-2 table rows are fuels, not tests: each cell gives the range or list of conditions covered for that fuel. `test_id` is the fuel as written; a combination of the listed values is not claimed to have been tested.
- **PSI-68**: FLEX-2 "Ambient Gas Mixture" reads "O2/N2 or O2/He": one `diluent` record per alternative (N2, He).
- **PSI-69**: FLEX composition headers read "O", "N", "CO", "He" (subscripts lost in the CSV). They are read as O2, N2, CO2 and He: the four fractions of every row sum to 1, and the FLEX Test Plan (`psi-data/PSI-69/Science Documents/PSI-69_Science Documents_FLEX_Test_Plan.pdf`, Table 8) writes "Fuel: C7H16 , Diluent: N2 , Suppressant: CO2" and "XN2 = 1.0 - XO2 - XCO2".
- **PSI-69**: FLEX `diluent` is derived per row from the N2, CO2 and He fractions: the single non-zero species, or `mixed` when N2 is combined with CO2 or He.
