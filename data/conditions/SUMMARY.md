## Table readings that rely on a judgement

Each of these is applied by code, the same way to every row, and is also listed in [`REVIEW.md`](REVIEW.md).

- **PSI-69**: FLEX composition headers read "O", "N", "CO", "He" (subscripts lost in the CSV). They are read as O2, N2, CO2 and He: the four fractions of every row sum to 1, and the FLEX Test Plan (`psi-data/PSI-69/Science Documents/PSI-69_Science Documents_FLEX_Test_Plan.pdf`, Table 8) writes "Fuel: C7H16 , Diluent: N2 , Suppressant: CO2" and "XN2 = 1.0 - XO2 - XCO2".
- **PSI-69**: FLEX `diluent` is derived per row from the N2, CO2 and He fractions: the single non-zero species, or `mixed` when N2 is combined with CO2 or He.
- **PSI-69**: FLEX "Burning rate; mm" is kept as `insufficient_evidence`: the unit written in the header (mm) is not a unit of burning rate, and the table does not state the right one.
