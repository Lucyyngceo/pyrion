# BASS-II PMMA rod tests: what the PSI files support about the flammability boundary

Generated by `extraction/build_derived.py` from `bass2_flow_steps.csv` and `bass2_rod_nasa_numbers.csv`. Interpreted columns were proposed by an extractor agent and audited by a separate verificador agent in two passes (segregated internal check by language models, not expert or NASA review). Nothing was tuned to match NASA's published numbers.

Sources of the two passes: pass 1 used only the PSI experimental table cells; pass 2 added the BASS-II Summary Report observation column (Tables A.3 and A.5, pp. 114-115 and 118-119) and the fan calibration workbook (`psi-data/PSI-25/Reference Data/PSI-25_Reference Data_Fan calibration data.xlsx`). Pass-1 columns are kept as they were.

## Forced stretch a_f = 3/2 · U / R

Formula and symbol from Olson & Ferkul 2015 (NTRS 20150008961, p.7): forced stretch of the rod tip a_f = 3/2 · U/R, U in cm/s and R the rod radius in cm. Computed here for each known end separately; no bracket is complete, so each value is an **upper bound of the stretch at blowoff**, not an interval.

## Comparison with NASA's published statements

Source: Olson & Ferkul 2015, NTRS 20150008961. Quotes with pages are in `bass2_rod_nasa_numbers.csv`.

- **24 concurrent-flow tests:** matches by count. The table has 27 Olson rod rows (O1..O22); the paper's 24 are the pre-2016 ones (22 clear and 2 black rods). Not an identification of every test.
- **Minimum O2 of 13.6 %:** matches: the lowest post-burn O2 among the limit tests is 13.600 % (O4).
- **Quench at low flow, blowoff at high flow:** consistent in words, not testable in numbers. All 7 cm/s values we can give are blowoff flows (upper ends 11 to 37 cm/s); the 24 quench limits sit at pot settings of about 0.26 to 0.38, below the lowest pot the calibration tabulates for a day, and interpolation was not allowed, so no quench flow is converted.
- **Flow boundary of the paper (Figure 4):** not reproduced. It was not digitized and nothing was tuned: with no complete bracket and one-sided blowoff bounds, the PSI files do not allow a flow-versus-oxygen boundary to be rebuilt from them.

## Limits

- Calibrations exist only for 2014 dates in the workbook; no 2016 test (O20-O22) has a calibration or a report observation. Dates 6/13, 6/16 and 7/30 have no block.
- Where the table cell and the report disagree on how a test ended, the cell-only label was kept as `unclear` (P19, P21a, O3a overshoot).
- The Summary Report observation column and the calibration workbook are PSI files, but they are second sources next to the table; their column alignment was read from a text layer.
- Details and the verificador's answers: `extraction/documents/review/derived-bass2-rods*.json`.
