# BASS-II PMMA rod tests: what the PSI table supports about the flammability boundary

Generated by `extraction/build_derived.py` from `bass2_flow_steps.csv` (interpreted columns) and `bass2_rod_nasa_numbers.csv`. The interpreted columns were proposed by an extractor agent and audited by a separate verificador agent (segregated internal check by language models, not expert or NASA review). Nothing was tuned to match NASA's published numbers.

## Counts

- Rod rows interpreted: **54** (all rows with `geometry_class = rod`; 50 have a radius, 4 labels have no inch mark and are kept as found).
- `limit_type`: quench **14**, blowoff **5**, none **2**, unclear **33**.
- Rods with a numeric flow in cm/s at the limit (`last_stable_flow_cm_s` or `extinction_flow_cm_s`): **0**.
- Tests with a usable limit type (quench or blowoff): **19**, of which with a flow in cm/s at that limit: **0**.

The lowest listed value, 0.600 % (P22a), is not a credible post-burn atmosphere for a test that burned (anomaly noted by the extractor, left as found). The lowest plausible one is **13.600 %** (O4).

## Comparison with NASA's published numbers

Source: Olson & Ferkul 2015, NTRS 20150008961 (`data/raw/nasa/ntrs-20150008961/`). Quotes with pages are in `bass2_rod_nasa_numbers.csv`.

- **24 concurrent-flow tests.** The table has 27 Olson rod rows (ids O1..O22, letters a/b are separate runs of one sample). The paper's 24 tests are the pre-2016 Olson rows (22 clear and 2 black rods; O20-O22 are 2016 tests and are not in the Summary Report's Table A.5), as the extractor found. This is a match by count and by the verificador's check, not an identification of every test.
- **13.6 % O2 minimum test point.** The lowest post-burn O2 of an Olson limit test in the table is O4 (13.6 %), which matches the paper's 'minimum test point of 13.6 % O2'. The paper's boundary intercept (13.3 % O2) is a fit and is not reproduced here.
- **Flow at the limit does NOT match.** NASA used the flow at which flames extinguished and the last stable flow (Figure 4, in cm/s from the daily fan calibration). In this table every quench or blowoff limit is a pot setting or fan-display value (e.g. '0.36 ext', 'blowoff pot 6.0') or has no number: **0** rods have a flow in cm/s at the limit. The paper's boundary points cannot be rebuilt from the PSI table cells. They need the daily fan-calibration data and the report's per-test observation column (Summary Report Table A.3/A.5), which are outside the condition records and were deliberately not used here.
- **Stretch rate.** The paper's forced stretch of the rod tip is a_f = 3/2 U/R. With no limit flow in cm/s for any rod, no stretch rate is computed.

## Limits

- 'unclear' rows stay unclear: the cell shows flows stepped, raised or alternated but never says the flame went out or blew off.
- The Summary Report's observation column states the outcome for many of these rows. It is not part of the PSI table and was not used to decide; if it is allowed as a second source, many 'unclear' rows would become quench or blowoff (the verificador counted 21 of the 29 that were unclear before its four corrections; see its file).
- Rows P5 and P13a read 'down to ext' with the fan turned to 0: whether they count as quench limit points is in the verificador's answer in `extraction/documents/review/derived-bass2-rods.verificador.json`.
