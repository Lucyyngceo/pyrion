What it is: The chart of stretch rate versus oxygen.

What it shows: Solid lines are PSI BASS-II rod tests; the hatched band is the hypothesised buoyant stretch at the selected gravity.

How to use it: Choose a formula and read where lines meet the band.

Example: Read how many tests overlap at the Moon.

What it does not do: The formulas are for curved bodies in flow; flat sheets are excluded.
