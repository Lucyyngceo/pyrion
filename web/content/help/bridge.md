In PYRION, gravity is a regime barrier: a microgravity test and a lunar-gravity target are not comparable. The Bridge page explores a labelled hypothesis from NASA literature: that partial gravity can be related to other gravity levels through flame stretch rate.

It points at PSI tests whose stretch rate is closest, with a gravity dial and a stretch map. It never changes a verdict, and nothing there predicts flame behaviour on the Moon.
