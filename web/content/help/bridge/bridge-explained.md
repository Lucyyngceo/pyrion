On the Envelope page, gravity is a regime barrier: a test in microgravity cannot be compared with a condition in lunar gravity, and the verdict says so. But NASA's own literature relates gravity levels through a quantity called **flame stretch rate**.

The Bridge page takes that idea and asks: in which PSI tests was the flame stretched by about as much as a flame would be by buoyancy on the Moon? Those tests are called **transfer analogs**.

It is a **hypothesis, not evidence**. It never changes a verdict and it does not predict how a flame behaves on the Moon.
