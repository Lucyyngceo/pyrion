FM2 is the Flammability of Materials on the Moon experiment. It burns samples in a closed chamber, and each burn uses up oxygen. The balance on the Bridge page answers a narrow question: how much oxygen can a burn use before the chamber falls to an assumed limiting oxygen concentration?

It is a **mass balance, not a flame model**. The chamber volume and the starting atmospheres come from NASA's plan. The temperature and the limit are assumptions you can change, shown with their ranges. It does not say where the real limit is.
