What it is: The first card.

What it shows: Stretch rate in one sentence, then NASA's own words about equivalence of flame stretch rates and the gravity values PYRION uses.

How to use it: Read it first.

Example: Moon 0.165 g, Mars 0.38 g, with sources.

What it does not do: It does not claim any prediction.
