Before any FM2 result exists, PYRION wrote down what it will and will not say about FM2: for example, that it commits to no value for the lunar limiting oxygen of the planned fabric, because PSI holds no test of it.

The fingerprint of that text, a SHA-256, was stamped with OpenTimestamps, which anchors it in the Bitcoin blockchain. When FM2 reports, anyone can check the statements against the results and check that the text did not change. The proof may show as pending until the blockchain confirms it.
