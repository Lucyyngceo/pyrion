Flame stretch rate describes how quickly the area of a flame surface changes relative to its area; its unit is per second. PYRION estimates a flow-related stretch rate using the formulas on the Bridge page. Forced airflow in microgravity and buoyancy at partial gravity can produce similar calculated stretch rates. Treat that comparison as a hypothesis, not evidence of identical burning.

The formulas used are for curved bodies in flow, such as a rod tip. Flat sheets have no radius, so no stretch is computed for them.
