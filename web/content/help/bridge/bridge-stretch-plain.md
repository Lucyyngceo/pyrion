title: Stretch rate, in plain words
chapter: bridge
order: 2
---
Stretch rate, in reciprocal seconds, is how hard the surrounding gas flow pulls on a flame. In orbit there is no buoyancy, so a weak forced airflow sets the stretch. On the Moon there is a little buoyancy. The two can stretch a flame by similar amounts. That is the bridge.

The formulas used are for curved bodies in flow, such as a rod tip. Flat sheets have no radius, so no stretch is computed for them.
