What it is: The coloured background.

What it shows: Bright cyan where investigations apply, violet tones for extrapolation by distance, dark where none covers.

How to use it: Read it to see where evidence is dense; hover to see which investigations apply.

Example: Look for the bright area around air.

What it does not do: The map is computed for the selected regime only.
