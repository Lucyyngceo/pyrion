What it is: The drawing area.

What it shows: Hulls, points, map shading and your target on oxygen and pressure axes.

How to use it: Click to place the target; drag on empty space to pan.

Example: Drag the marker to move the target.

What it does not do: A click on a point opens that test's receipt instead of moving the target.
