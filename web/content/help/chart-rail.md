What it is: The thin coloured strip at the chart's right edge.

What it shows: The headline at your oxygen along the pressure axis.

How to use it: Read where along pressure the result changes.

Example: Find where it turns from green to amber.

What it does not do: It is computed for your oxygen and regime only.
