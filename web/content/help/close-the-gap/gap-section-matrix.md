What it is: A table of proposed test points.

What it shows: Oxygen and pressure steps around the target; margin points are marked.

How to use it: Use it to see how many tests would build an envelope.

Example: 15 points around 34 % and 8.2 psia.

What it does not do: The pressure steps of 15 % are a PYRION design convention, not NASA's.
