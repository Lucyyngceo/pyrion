What it is: A small button on a tile.

What it shows: Opens a dialog with the engine's plan of experiments that would add evidence for that scenario.

How to use it: Click it to see the test matrix, the measurements and what FM2 and LUCI plan.

Example: Click it on NASA's Moon Base.

What it does not do: It is a PYRION proposal, not a NASA plan.
