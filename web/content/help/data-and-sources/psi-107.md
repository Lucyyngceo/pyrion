PSI-107 SPICE is a NASA microgravity investigation in the PSI (Physical Sciences Informatics) archive: “Smoke Point In Co-flow Experiment”. Release 18-Sep-2024, version 4. DOI [10.60555/9kbh-e962](https://doi.org/10.60555/9kbh-e962). License: CC0-1.0.

In plain words: this page says what PSI-107 looked at, what conditions PYRION could read from it (with the place in NASA's files where each one is written), and what it could not read. 913 of its 924 records are usable as evidence.

## What it studied
PSI objective (quoted, section `objective`):

> The objective of SPICE is to better understand the complex process of combustion by observing flames in a microgravity environment aboard the International Space Station (ISS) and analogous ground experiments. Specific objectives were: (1) Identify the laminar smoke-point properties of non-buoyant jet diffusion flames under various co-flow conditions in microgravity. (2) Determine the effects of burner diameter and co-flow velocity on the luminous flame lengths and smoke points. (3) Quantify the sooting propensity of different fuels, such as ethylene, propane, and propylene mixtures. [...]

PSI hypothesis (quoted, section `hypothesis`):

> The research hypothesizes that by minimizing gravity-driven transport phenomena scientists can study the laminar smoke-point properties of non-buoyant jet diffusion flames. Flame properties are expected to differ significantly from those observed under normal gravity.

As recorded by PSI: flight program: International Space Station (ISS); hardware: Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG); managing center: Glenn Research Center (GRC).

## What could not be extracted, and why
Records kept as `insufficient_evidence` carry their reason in the `note` field. The most common ones:

- `flow_velocity` (8): negative air rate: not a possible co-flow speed as written
- `o2_mole_fraction` (1): Section 'SPICE FLIGHT TESTS' (printed page 13). This is the band within which ISS cabin air is kept, not a per-test O2 measurement. No O2 measurement inside the MSG is reported.
- `o2_mole_fraction` (1): confidence ambiguous (Footnote a of Table 1. The words left out are ', uf,'. The PDF text layer splits the subscripted u_f across lines. [...]
- `pressure` (1): Same footnote as R10. 101 kPa is the pressure ASSUMED for the velocity and Reynolds-number calculations. No sentence in the sources read states the actual MSG or cabin pressure during the tests (see not_found).
