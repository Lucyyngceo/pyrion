These are the NASA pages and documents linked from the Flame in Freefall challenge, plus two NASA images. PYRION saved them as background: the history of spacecraft fires (Apollo 1, Apollo 13, Mir) and NASA's own descriptions of its combustion science.

In plain words: they explain why the work matters and give context. They are not test data. Apollo and Mir documents are history, not conditions that PYRION extracts.

The list (address and what each is used for):

- NASA image: edge view of SIBAL (nasa-img-sibal): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/edge-view-of-sibal.png (role: image: SIBAL fabric burning in simulated lunar gravity (FM2 page))
