FM2 (Flammability of Materials on the Moon) is a planned NASA experiment to burn materials at lunar gravity on the Moon. PYRION holds two NASA sources about it: the NASA Glenn web page and the 2026 Lunar and Planetary Science Conference presentation by Ferkul and colleagues (NTRS 20260001966).

In plain words: FM2 is the reason the app has a "Moon base" question. It is a plan. It has produced no measurement yet, so PYRION uses it to say what will be tested, never as evidence that a material does or does not burn.

What the NASA page says (quoted):

> FM2 investigates burning materials on the Moon at its 1/6 reduced gravity level.

> The experiment burns four fuel samples individually in atmospheric conditions planned for future lunar habitats or vehicles.

What the presentation says about the plan (quoted from PDF page 5, printed "Page 4"):

> Test a material with an expected Lunar LOC of 24-26% and an expected oxygen consumption of 0.08 mol fraction

> Conduct four tests at 8.2 psi: 1. Start at 37% should burn to threshold 2. Start at 33% should burn to threshold 3. Start at 29% should burn to threshold 4. Start at 25% not expected to ignite but would provide a margin of safety if the limit was lower than expected.

These are expectations, not results. In the app, the FM2 hypothesised limit slider on the Bridge page is a hypothesis, not a measurement.
