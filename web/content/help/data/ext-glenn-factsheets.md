NASA Glenn publishes one fact sheet per flight combustion experiment. PYRION keeps eight of them next to the PSI data to explain what each experiment is.

In plain words: these are short NASA descriptions that help explain the PSI investigations. Some quoted fact-sheet descriptions support regime records; they do not supply missing per-test oxygen or pressure measurements. The receipt identifies a fact sheet whenever it is used.

The eight fact sheets:
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Advanced Combustion via Microgravity Experiments (ACME): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/advanced-combustion-via-microgravity-experiments-acme.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Burning and Suppression of Solids (BASS): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/burning-and-suppression-of-solids-bass.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Dust and Aerosol Measurement Feasibility Test (DAFT): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/dust-and-aerosol-measurement-feasibility-test-daft.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Flame Extinguishment Experiment (FLEX): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/flame-extinguishment-experiment-flex.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Spacecraft Fire Safety Demonstration (Saffire): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/spacecraft-fire-safety-demonstration-saffire.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment (SAME): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/smoke-aerosol-measurement-experiment-same.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Structure and Liftoff in Combustion Experiment (SLICE): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/structure-liftoff-in-combustion-experiment-slice.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
- NASA Glenn fact sheet: Smoke Point In Co-flow Experiment (SPICE): https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/smoke-point-in-co-flow-experiment-spice.pdf (role: experiment fact sheet)
