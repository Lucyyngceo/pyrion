PSI-101 SAME-R is a NASA microgravity investigation in the PSI (Physical Sciences Informatics) archive: “Smoke Aerosol Measurement Experiment-Reflight”. Release 18-Sep-2024, version 4. DOI [10.60555/2np7-jv14](https://doi.org/10.60555/2np7-jv14). License: CC0-1.0.

In plain words: this topic explains PSI-101, its cited sources and extraction limits. The [investigation sheet](/investigations/PSI-101) and [Data page](/data) provide the current records, statuses and coverage.

## What it studied
PSI objective (quoted, section `objective`):

> Similar to the original SAME investigation, the primary objective of the SAME-R investigation was to quantify the sizes that the liquid smoke particulate achieves in low gravity and to relate the droplet size distribution to the smoke generation and smoke transport conditions in the experiment. One of the problems encountered during the SAME investigation was that after the thermal precipitators were returned to the ground, it was found that all the TEM grids were severely contaminated with some unidentified foreign particles, making these grids unusable although some of them showed shapes of certain types of smoke particles. [...]

PSI hypothesis (quoted, section `hypothesis`):

> The three hypotheses that motivated the original SAME investigation is also applicable for the reflight SAME-R investigation: In a μg environment, with the absence of the strong buoyancy-induced flows, smoke particle growth from a pyrolyzing/smoldering source, as a result of nucleation, condensation, and coagulation, will lead to larger particles compared to those produced in 1g. The formation of the larger particles is favored by the expected higher vapor concentration and smoke residence times near the source due to the reduced velocities and mixing at μg conditions. [...]

As recorded by PSI: flight program: International Space Station (ISS); hardware: Comparitive Soot Diagnostic Hardware; Refer to final report; managing center: Glenn Research Center (GRC).

## Platform and gravity, as stated
- Platform: **International Space Station (ISS)** (quote: “International Space Station (ISS)”, `section=flightProgram`).
- Platform: **Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG)** (quote: “An unfortunate side effect was that approximately half the smoke was lost from the system because it diffused out of the inlet into the MSG volume.”, `page=9`).
- Gravity regime: **microgravity** for test(s) `*` (quote: “Key parameters affecting the smoke properties of the smoke produced here are the low-gravity condition, the convective flow past the heated wire, the wire temperature, and the aging time.”, `page=8`, PSI-101_Reports_SAME-R_Final Report.pdf).

fuel phase: solid; diluent: N2; material descriptions: Kapton, Silicone, Lampwick, Pyrell, Teflon.
