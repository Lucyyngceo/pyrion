PSI-102 SAME is a NASA microgravity investigation in the PSI (Physical Sciences Informatics) archive: “Smoke and Aerosol Measurement Experiment”. Release 18-Sep-2024, version 4. DOI [10.60555/gd4k-8h62](https://doi.org/10.60555/gd4k-8h62). License: CC0-1.0.

In plain words: this topic explains PSI-102, its cited sources and extraction limits. The [investigation sheet](/investigations/PSI-102) and [Data page](/data) provide the current records, statuses and coverage.

## What it studied
PSI objective (quoted, section `objective`):

> One of the major goals of this investigation was to quantify the sizes that the liquid smoke particulate achieves in low gravity and to relate the droplet size distribution to the smoke generation and smoke transport conditions in the experiment. A numerical code was to be developed to predict the smoke droplet growth as a function of the fuel pyrolysis rate, the thermodynamic properties of the pyrolysis vapor, and the flow environment.

PSI hypothesis (quoted, section `hypothesis`):

> The three hypotheses that motivated this investigation: In a μg environment, with the absence of the strong buoyancy-induced flows, smoke particle growth from a pyrolyzing/smoldering source, as a result of nucleation, condensation, and coagulation, will lead to larger particles compared to those produced in 1g. The formation of the larger particles is favored by the expected higher vapor concentration and smoke residence times near the source due to the reduced velocities and mixing at μg conditions. [...]

As recorded by PSI: flight program: International Space Station (ISS); hardware: Comparitive Soot Diagnostic Hardware; managing center: Glenn Research Center (GRC).

## Platform and gravity, as stated
- Platform: **International Space Station (ISS)** (quote: “International Space Station (ISS)”, `section=flightProgram`).
- Platform: **Microgravity Science Glovebox** (quote: “The system was installed in the Microgravity Science Glovebox, an ISS facility that provides many resources including: containment, power, data, video and uplink commanding.”, `page=20`).
- Gravity regime: **microgravity** for test(s) `*` (quote: “Smoke particle size measurements were obtained under low-gravity conditions by overheating several materials typical of those found in spacecraft.”, `page=43`, PSI-102_Reports_SAME_Final Science Report.pdf).
- Gravity regime: **microgravity** for test(s) `1` (quote: “Smoke particulate produced in low-gravity was found to be typically 50% large in count mean diameter than similar conditions in normal gravity.”, `page=66`, PSI-102_Reports_SAME_Final Science Report.pdf).

## Fuel and flame configuration
fuel phase: solid, liquid; diluent: N2; material descriptions: Teflon, Kapton, Silicone Rubber, Lampwick, Dibutyl Phthalate.
