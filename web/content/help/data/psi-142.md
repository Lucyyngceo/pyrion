PSI-142 is a NASA ground investigation in the PSI (Physical Sciences Informatics) archive: “Quantitative Studies of Cool Flame Transitions at Radiation / Stretch Extinction Using Counterflow Flames”. Release 18-Sep-2024, version 2. DOI [10.60555/80qf-vj95](https://doi.org/10.60555/80qf-vj95). License: CC0-1.0.

In plain words: this page says what PSI-142 looked at and why PYRION holds no condition values from it.

## What it studied
PSI objective (quoted, section `objective`):

> By using the Flame Extinguishment (FLEX) experimental data from the Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) system of combustion science, the purpose of this grant was to conduct new simulations and ground-based experiments using ozone-sensitized counterflow flames to: 1) Develop a new diagram of the flammability limits of diffusional cool flames affected by radiation, diffusion, and convection. 2) Bridge the knowledge gap between the flammability limits and extinction limits of hot flames and cool flames. 3) Verify the phenomena observed on the ISS flame extinguishment experiments. [...]

PSI hypothesis (quoted, section `hypothesis`):

> This investigation attempts to answer the critical question as to what conditions causes the hot flames transfer to cool flames and how the cool flame flammable region is related to hot flame flammability limit and extinction limit. The investigation hypothesizes that factors such as oxygen concentration, dilution, pressure, diffusion, and convection transport processes affect the cool flame transition.

As recorded by PSI: flight program: Not Applicable; hardware: Principal Investigator Laboratory Hardware; managing center: Glenn Research Center (GRC).
