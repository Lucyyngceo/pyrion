PSI-60 is a NASA ground investigation in the PSI (Physical Sciences Informatics) archive: “Computational and Experimental Analysis of SPICE Data Sets”. Release 30-Sep-2025, version 1. DOI [10.60555/mzjm-yv57](https://doi.org/10.60555/mzjm-yv57). License: CC0-1.0.

In plain words: this page says what PSI-60 looked at and why PYRION holds no condition values from it.

## What it studied
PSI objective (quoted, section `objective`):

> This ground investigation sought to increase understanding of the issues that control the large discrepancy between soot formation in 1g and in 0g flames. The objective of this investigation was to integrate computational and experimental methods to derive temperature distributions and soot volume fractions from the Smoke Point in Coflow Experiment (SPICE) data available in PSI. By analyzing over 1600 raw DSLR images and incorporating advanced soot models, the study sought to improve understanding of soot formation, oxidation, radiative re-absorption, and aggregate growth in heavily sooting flames. [...]

PSI hypothesis (quoted, section `hypothesis`):

> Microgravity conditions would produce longer, more diffuse flames with lower temperatures and increased soot concentrations compared to normal gravity, and that careful integration of experimental temperature fields into the simulations would reduce discrepancies between measured and predicted soot distributions.

As recorded by PSI: managing center: Glenn Research Center (GRC).

This is a statement about what PSI holds for this investigation and what the extraction rules allow, not about the science. An investigation with no usable record never moves a verdict: the engine reads it as “not enough evidence”.
