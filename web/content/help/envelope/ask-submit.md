What it is: Not shown on the site today: the Ask TARS SI bar and its button were removed from the Envelope page; ask in the TARS SI bubble at the bottom right instead. This topic describes the former button.

What it shows: Nothing by itself.

How to use it: Press it to open TARS SI and send your question.

Example: Type a question in the TARS SI bubble and press Send.

What it does not do: It does not change the chart; use Show on the envelope in TARS's answer for that.
