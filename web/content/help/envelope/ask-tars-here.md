What it is: A small pink button with a spark on scenario tiles, verdict cards, routes, blockers and the sections of the plan of what would close the gap.

What it shows: Nothing by itself; it opens TARS SI and sends a question that already says what you are looking at, with the page context.

How to use it: Click it, then read the answer in the TARS panel. You can follow up in the same conversation.

Example: Click it on NASA's Moon Base to ask why PSI holds no test for it.

What it does not do: It does not change the page, and TARS's answer is not a verdict: verdicts come only from the engine.
