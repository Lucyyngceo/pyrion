What it is: The small label next to the target.

What it shows: The scenario name and the headline: inside or outside the measured envelope.

How to use it: Read it for a one-glance result.

Example: "Inside the measured envelope".

What it does not do: "Inside" means a PSI test covered it in this regime; it does not mean safe.
