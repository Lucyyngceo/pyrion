What it is: The card that holds the chart and its controls.

What it shows: The legend, the chart, the target callout, the map legend and a data table.

How to use it: Use its buttons to zoom, pan or open it full screen.

Example: Click Full screen to see the sidebar.

What it does not do: It is a view of the extracted records; it does not add data.
