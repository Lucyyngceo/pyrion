What it is: The shaded polygons.

What it shows: For each investigation, the smallest outline holding its measured oxygen and pressure points.

How to use it: Check whether your target marker is inside an outline of the right colour.

Example: Move the target into a green-teal outline.

What it does not do: Inside an outline in a different regime does not count: regimes are barriers.
