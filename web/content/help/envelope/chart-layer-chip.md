What it is: A chip for one regime combination (gravity, fuel phase, diluent, flame type).

What it shows: Its colour, its marker shape (solid square, liquid circle, gas triangle) and its point count.

How to use it: Click to hide or show the layer.

Example: Hide "microgravity · liquid fuel".

What it does not do: A hollow marker means some regime axis is not stated in the extracted records.
