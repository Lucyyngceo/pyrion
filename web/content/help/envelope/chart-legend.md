What it is: The chips above the chart.

What it shows: One chip per regime layer with its number of points, plus the view and zoom buttons.

How to use it: Click a chip to hide or show that layer.

Example: Hide the gas-fuel layer.

What it does not do: Hiding a layer is a view choice; it does not change verdicts.
