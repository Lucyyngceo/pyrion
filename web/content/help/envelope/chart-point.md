What it is: A single mark (hover target).

What it shows: The test id, the oxygen, the pressure and its regime in the tooltip.

How to use it: Hover for details; click to open the investigation card with receipts.

Example: Click a point to jump to its card.

What it does not do: It does not prove that other conditions between points burn the same way.
