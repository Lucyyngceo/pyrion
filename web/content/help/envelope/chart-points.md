What it is: The dots, triangles and squares on the chart.

What it shows: One mark per extracted oxygen and pressure pair of a test.

How to use it: Hover for the test, click for its receipt.

Example: Hover a point to see its investigation.

What it does not do: A line joining two points is a measured range, not interpolation.
