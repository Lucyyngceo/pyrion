What it is: The oxygen-versus-pressure picture of what PSI tested.

What it shows: Shaded outlines per regime, points per test, shading per map tone, and your target.

How to use it: Click it to move the target, drag to pan, Ctrl and wheel to zoom.

Example: Click at 30 % and 70 kPa.

What it does not do: It plots only values extracted so far; investigations without extracted values are absent.
