What it is: The small Ctrl K key hint at the bottom right of every page.

What it shows: A button that opens a jump-to box.

How to use it: Click it, or press Ctrl K, to open the palette.

Example: Press Ctrl K and type "bridge".

What it does not do: It does not search NASA documents; for that, ask TARS.
