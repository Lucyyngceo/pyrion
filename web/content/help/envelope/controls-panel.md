What it is: The left column of controls.

What it shows: Oxygen, pressure, gravity regime and the other axes.

How to use it: Change any control to test your own condition.

Example: Move pressure to 70 kPa.

What it does not do: Changing a control leaves the preset: the result then says "your condition".
