The **Full screen** button opens the chart over the whole window with a sidebar listing where the evidence is. **Esc** returns you to the page, with the same view.

**Pan** by dragging anywhere on the plot. **Move the target** by dragging from its marker, or by clicking without moving. **Zoom** with the plus and minus buttons, with the wheel in full screen, or with Ctrl and the wheel on the page. The arrow keys pan, plus and minus zoom, and 0 resets.

The axes always show their units and add psia once the view is zoomed. Zoom and pan change only what you see; no verdict changes.

Open the [dashboard](/envelope) to use these controls.
