Behind the outlines and points the chart is shaded. The shading is the evidence map of the regime you selected.

**Bright cyan** means that at least one investigation applies there: it tested the same regime and covers that oxygen and pressure. The brighter, the more investigations.

**Three violet tones** mark extrapolation by distance from the tested data: just outside, outside and far outside. They are steps of distance, not degrees of safety.

**A hatch** marks a combination that was never tested although each setting was tested on its own.

**Dark** means no investigation of this regime covers the point.

Hover the chart to read which investigations apply at the cursor.
