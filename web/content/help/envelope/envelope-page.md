The Envelope page is the home page. The chart plots oxygen (% by volume) against pressure (kPa). Each shaded outline is the envelope of one regime: the oxygen and pressure range that PSI tested for one combination of gravity, fuel phase, diluent and flame type.

Your condition is the target marker. Click the chart, drag the marker or use the controls on the left to move it. Dragging elsewhere pans the view; Ctrl and the wheel, or the + and - buttons, zoom.

Inside an outline means a test covered that oxygen and pressure. Outside means no extracted PSI test did.
