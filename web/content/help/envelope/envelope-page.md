The Envelope page at /envelope is the dashboard. The chart plots oxygen (% by volume) against pressure (kPa). Each shaded outline is the envelope of one regime: the oxygen and pressure range that PSI tested for one combination of gravity, fuel phase, diluent and flame type.

Your condition is the target marker. Click the chart, drag the marker or use the controls on the left to move it. Dragging elsewhere pans the view; Ctrl and the wheel, or the + and - buttons, zoom.

Inside an outline means that oxygen and pressure lie within a region spanned by measured points of one investigation in one regime, subject to the 0.5% tolerance. It does not mean a test ran at that exact point. Being outside one outline does not mean being outside every investigation's region; read the engine verdicts for your selected axes.
