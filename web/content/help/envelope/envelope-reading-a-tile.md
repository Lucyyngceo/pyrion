Take NASA's Moon Base.

**The title** names the scenario, and the line under it says where it comes from. **The condition line** gives oxygen, pressure and gravity with units. A pressure the source writes as "psi" stays "psi", with a hover note that it is absolute pressure.

**The bold line** says how many investigations apply and how many need extrapolation, or, when none applies, the headline of the chart. For the Moon Base it reads "No PSI test in this gravity regime".

**The smaller lines** give more: the closest solid-fuel investigation that can be compared and how many others lack the values, or, for the exploration atmosphere, what microgravity would give for the same atmosphere.

**The quote** on the Moon Base tile is from NASA's FM2 page, with a link. **The button** What would close this gap opens the plan of experiments (chapter 5). Hover a tile for its sources.
