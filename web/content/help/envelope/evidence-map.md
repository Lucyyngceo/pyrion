Under the chart's outlines, brighter cyan means more applicable investigations in the selected regime. Violet tones distinguish extrapolation distances. Hatching marks areas outside the measured hulls for that regime; hover text explains the applicable verdict. Dark areas have no applicable investigation.

Hover the chart to see which investigations apply at the cursor.
