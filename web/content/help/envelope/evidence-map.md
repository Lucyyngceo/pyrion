Under the chart's outlines, the shading is the evidence map for the selected regime. Bright cyan means NASA investigations apply there; brighter means more of them. Violet means only extrapolation, in three steps by distance from the tested data (just outside, outside, far outside), with a hatch for an untested combination. Dark means no investigation of that regime covers the point.

Hover the chart to see which investigations apply at the cursor.
