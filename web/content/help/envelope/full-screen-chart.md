The Full screen button opens the chart over the whole window with a sidebar listing where the evidence is. Esc or "Exit full screen" returns to the page with the same view.

Drag anywhere to pan. Drag from the target marker to move the target. A click without movement places the target. In full screen the wheel zooms around the cursor; on the page, hold Ctrl. The arrow keys pan, + and - zoom, 0 resets. Zoom and pan change only the view, never a verdict.
