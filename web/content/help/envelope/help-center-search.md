What it is: The search field of the Help center.

What it shows: Your words, matched against titles, keywords and text of every topic.

How to use it: Type a word such as zoom.

Example: Type "extrapolation".

What it does not do: It is a keyword search, not an AI search; ask TARS for a conversation.
