What it is: The read-only transfer_hypothesis tool exposed by PYRION's MCP server.

What it shows: BASS-II rod tests whose calculated flow-related stretch rates fit the calculated buoyant-stretch band at a selected gravity, with the assumptions and limits.

How to use it: Connect an MCP client using the panel at the top of the dashboard, then ask it to call transfer_hypothesis and retain the hypothesis label.

Example: Ask which BASS-II rod tests have similar calculated stretch rates at Moon gravity.

What it does not do: It does not change applicability verdicts or establish that a flame will behave the same way at another gravity.
