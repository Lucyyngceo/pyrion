What it is: The home page of PYRION: scenario tiles, the evidence chart, the controls and the verdict.

What it shows: The page you are on when its tab is highlighted.

How to use it: Click it to open the Envelope page. Everything about a condition happens here.

Example: After reading the Method page, click Envelope to try a condition.

What it does not do: It does not recompute anything by itself; it only shows the page.
