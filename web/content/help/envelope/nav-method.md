What it is: The page that explains how PYRION decides: axes, the four verdicts, ranking, the receipt, the checks and the role of the language model.

What it shows: Plain-language rules with the numbers that the engine uses.

How to use it: Click it when you want to know why a verdict says what it says.

Example: Read "The four verdicts" before comparing two investigations.

What it does not do: It does not run the engine; it documents it.
