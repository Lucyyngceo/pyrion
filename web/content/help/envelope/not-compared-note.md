What it is: A note under the chart that states what a verdict means and what it leaves out.

What it shows: That a verdict compares recorded conditions, and that "inside the tested range" does not mean a test was run at this exact condition. It lists what is never compared: material or fuel composition within a phase, sample geometry and orientation, flow direction and configuration, and ignition source.

How to use it: Read it before relying on a verdict. If material, geometry or flow configuration matter for your question, open the investigation and check its tests yourself.

Example: A condition inside the tested range of an investigation that burned a different material than yours is still reported inside the tested range; the material was not compared.

What it does not do: It does not change any verdict and it does not predict how a flame behaves.
