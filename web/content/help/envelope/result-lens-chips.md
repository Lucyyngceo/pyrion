What it is: Small chips on a result card.

What it shows: Which fire-safety legs (prevention, detection, suppression) the investigation has a quoted statement for, and how directly the challenge text ties it to spacecraft fire safety (direct, fundamental or indirect).

How to use it: Hover a chip group for what it means; read it next to the verdict.

Example: An investigation tagged detection and relevance: direct.

What it does not do: It does not change any verdict, and it is not a safety assessment. The tags are context taken from quoted sentences; they were not reviewed by NASA.
