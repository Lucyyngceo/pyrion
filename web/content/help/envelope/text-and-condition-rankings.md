What it is: The dashboard section comparing searches by wording with comparison by recorded test conditions.

What it shows: Lists headed By text similarity, By embedding search and By tested conditions. Text-based searches can put an investigation first even when its physical verdict is insufficient evidence or not comparable.

How to use it: Read each row's verdict as well as its position. Use the engine results to assess applicability.

Example: An investigation can match the words cabin fire while lacking the pressure needed for a comparison.

What it does not do: A high text or embedding score does not establish applicability, scientific importance or safety.
