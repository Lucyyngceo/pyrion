What it is: The monospaced line of numbers on a tile.

What it shows: Oxygen, pressure and gravity of the scenario, with units. A pressure the source writes as "psi" stays "psi" with a hover note that it is absolute.

How to use it: Read it to compare scenarios at a glance.

Example: "34 % O₂ · 8.2 psia · 0.16 g".

What it does not do: It is the condition PYRION tests against PSI, not a prediction about it.
