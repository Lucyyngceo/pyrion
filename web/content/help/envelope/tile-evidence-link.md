What it is: A link on the selected tile.

What it shows: Jumps to the list of investigations for that condition, one card each, where every value opens its NASA source.

How to use it: Tap or click it after choosing a tile; on a phone it is the shortest way from the result to its evidence.

Example: Choose ISS cabin air, then follow the link to see which investigations were compared and why.

What it does not do: It does not change the condition or the verdict; it only scrolls to them.
