What it is: A smaller line below the summary on a tile.

What it shows: A computed fact such as the closest solid-fuel investigation, or what microgravity would give for the same atmosphere.

How to use it: Read it for the next level of detail.

Example: "Closest solid-fuel investigation that can be compared: BASS, just outside the tested data".

What it does not do: It names only investigations the engine could compare; the others are counted, not guessed.
