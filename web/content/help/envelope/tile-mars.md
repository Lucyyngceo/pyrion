What it is: A small link in the exploration atmosphere tile.

What it shows: The same NESC exploration atmosphere (34 % oxygen at 8.2 psia) at Mars gravity, 0.38 g, from the NASA NSSDC Mars Fact Sheet.

How to use it: Click it to load that condition.

Example: Compare the Moon and Mars pairings of the same atmosphere.

What it does not do: No source states a Mars habitat atmosphere; the pairing is PYRION's, and PSI holds no partial-gravity combustion data.
