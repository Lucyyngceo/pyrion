What it is: The title and condition of a tile, which is also the button that loads it.

What it shows: The scenario name and its condition line.

How to use it: Click it to apply that scenario to the controls and the chart.

Example: Click "NASA's Moon Base".

What it does not do: Loading a scenario replaces the current controls; Your condition shows them again if you change anything.
