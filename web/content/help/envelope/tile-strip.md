What it is: The row of four scenario tiles near the top of the page.

What it shows: ISS cabin air, NASA's Moon Base, NASA exploration atmosphere and Your condition, each with live numbers.

How to use it: Click a tile to load it into the dashboard; hover it for its sources.

Example: Click the exploration atmosphere tile.

What it does not do: The numbers are computed by the engine for exactly that condition; they are never typed in.
