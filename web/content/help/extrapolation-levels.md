An Extrapolation verdict also says how far outside the tested data your condition is: just outside, outside or far outside. A fourth level, "inside each tested range but this combination was not tested", is shown with a hatch on the map.

The levels measure distance from the tested data. They do not measure how reliable or safe the condition is. Combustion can change abruptly near limits, so even "just outside" is an extrapolation, never a pass.
