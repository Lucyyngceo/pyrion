What it is: A card with a closed-chamber oxygen balance.

What it shows: How much oxygen a burn can use before the chamber reaches a hypothesised limit, using FM2's stated volume and start conditions.

How to use it: Move the two sliders.

Example: Set a limit of 25 %.

What it does not do: It is a mass balance, not a flame model; it does not say where the limit is.
