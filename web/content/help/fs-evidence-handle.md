What it is: The bar at the bottom of the full-screen chart on narrow screens.

What it shows: The title "Where the evidence is" with an arrow.

How to use it: Tap it to open or close the evidence sheet.

Example: Open it on a phone.

What it does not do: It is the same list as the right column.
