What it is: A list of axes the experimenter chooses.

What it shows: Gravity level, flow velocity and sample thickness, with sources where they exist.

How to use it: Read it before planning.

Example: Gravity 0.165 g from FM2's centrifuge setting.

What it does not do: The engine does not choose these values.
