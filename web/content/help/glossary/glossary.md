Regime: the physical situation (gravity, fuel phase, diluent, flame type). Different regimes are not compared.

Convex hull: the smallest outline containing an investigation's measured oxygen and pressure points; inside it counts as tested.

Diluent: the gas that fills most of the atmosphere besides oxygen. Normal flame: fuel on one side, oxygen from the surrounding gas. Inverse flame: oxygen from a burner into a surrounding fuel gas.

psia is absolute pressure; kPa is shown first, psia in brackets.
