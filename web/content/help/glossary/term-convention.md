A choice PYRION made that is **not a NASA value** and is labelled as such wherever it affects a result: the 0.5 % tolerance, the 0.01 g microgravity cut, the 0.9 to 1.1 g normal band, the 10 % and 30 % extrapolation levels, the plausibility windows, and the test-matrix steps for pressure.

## For engineers

`MEASUREMENT_TOLERANCE_NOTE`, `MICROGRAVITY_CONVENTION_NOTE`, `NORMAL_GRAVITY_CONVENTION_NOTE`, `EXTRAPOLATION_LEVEL_NOTE`, `TEST_MATRIX_NOTE`, `HABITAT_WINDOWS` (see "Limits and honesty" for the list).
