The NASA Technical Reports Server: where NASA's published papers, presentations and reports live. PYRION cites them by NTRS number (for example NTRS 20260001966) and by PDF page.

## For engineers

NTRS record numbers identify entries in the NASA Technical Reports Server. A report may also have a DOI. Cite the identifier provided by the source. PYRION page references use PDF page numbers, with a printed slide number where stated.
