NASA's Physical Sciences Informatics archive of microgravity and combustion experiments. PYRION only knows what it extracted from PSI records, so it always says "in PSI" and never "no data exists".

## For engineers

Records come from the PSI investigations listed in `data/manifest/investigations.csv`; PYRION text uses "in PSI" by rule (`engine/src/answer.ts`).
