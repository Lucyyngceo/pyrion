What it is: Compares attributes from two or three PSI records.

What it shows: Hardware, test counts and extracted condition ranges side by side; observed differences are highlighted.

How to use it: Enter two or three accession IDs separated by commas, then select Compare.

Example: Enter PSI-25,PSI-26 to compare those records.

What it does not do: A highlighted difference is a text difference, not a verdict about applicability.
