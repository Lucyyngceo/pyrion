What it is: Filters the investigation list by text, fuel phase, or presence of extracted flow values.

What it shows: Matching investigations, their hardware text, and test counts from the extracted condition files.

How to use it: Enter text, select a fuel phase, or choose the flow filter; open a result to see its sources.

Example: Choose an available filter to inspect the records.

What it does not do: Filtering does not establish whether an investigation applies to a target condition.
