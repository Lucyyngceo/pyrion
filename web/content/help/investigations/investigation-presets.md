What it is: Links to comparisons of related PSI investigations.

What it shows: The selected investigations side by side.

How to use it: Choose a group, or enter two to six accession IDs manually.

Example: Choose BASS vs BASS-II.

What it does not do: A difference in the table does not establish applicability.
