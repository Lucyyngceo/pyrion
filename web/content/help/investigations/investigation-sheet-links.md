What it is: Links from an investigation sheet to related resources.

What it shows: Links to the investigation list, extracted dataset, publication DOIs, PYRION, and TARS.

How to use it: Select a link to open its destination.

Example: Select a publication DOI to open the cited publication.

What it does not do: These links do not confirm that an investigation applies to a target condition. Observed variation does not establish experimental design.
