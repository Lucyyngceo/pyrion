What it is: Links next to each quoted Earth sentence on the More page: the investigation sheet and the PSI DOI.

What it shows: The investigation whose PSI record states the quoted sentence.

How to use it: Select the investigation name to open its sheet, or the DOI to open the PSI record.

Example: Open the BASS-II link to read its full research impacts field.

What it does not do: The sentences are what the PSI record states; PYRION does not check or extend them.
