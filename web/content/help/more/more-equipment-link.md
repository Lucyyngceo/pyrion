What it is: The links on the three cards at the top of the More page (detection, suppression, prevention): one link per PSI investigation the card is about, one link to the lens matrix filtered by that safety leg, and a link to the challenge page.

What it shows: Each card is made only of quotes, each followed by its source: a PSI record and field (for example PSI-69, field objective) or the NASA Space Apps 2026 challenge statement. A quote marked "part of a sentence" is a word-for-word part of one sentence of the challenge statement.

How to use it: Select an investigation name to open its sheet, or "Every investigation tagged ... in the lens matrix" to see all investigations whose PSI record has a quote for that leg.

Example: On the detection card, open SAME to read its PSI record, then open the detection lens to see which other investigations mention detection.

What it does not do: It does not rate any equipment, recommend a design or say how well a detector or an extinguisher works. The cards repeat what PSI and the challenge statement say; PYRION adds no claim of its own.
