What it is: The page about the flame-stretch-rate hypothesis that relates gravity levels.

What it shows: A gravity dial, a stretch map of PSI tests and the FM2 oxygen balance. It is labelled a hypothesis.

How to use it: Click it to explore how a lunar-gravity condition could be related to PSI tests.

Example: Move the gravity dial to the Moon and look at which PSI tests have a similar stretch rate.

What it does not do: Nothing there changes a verdict on the Envelope page, and nothing there predicts flame behaviour on the Moon.
