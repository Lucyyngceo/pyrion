What it is: The first page: the question PYRION answers, what it found for NASA's Moon Base, and links to every part of the site.

What it shows: A top bar with the page sections and an "Explore the dashboard" button. The headline "Has NASA tested fire in your cabin air? See where the evidence ends.", a short explanation, and two buttons: "Explore the dashboard" and "See a worked example". Below it, the section "What PYRION found for NASA's Moon Base", computed by the fixed-rule engine, with try-it buttons (ISS cabin air, the Moon Base condition, Plan lunar tests). Further down: why it matters, quoted from NASA's challenge; what NASA set out to learn, quoted from PSI records; how it works; who it is for; links to every part of the site; and common questions.

How to use it: Click "Explore the dashboard" to open the Envelope with the Moon Base condition already set, or "See a worked example" to read one full case step by step.

Example: Click "Explore the dashboard" and read which PSI tests apply to the Moon Base air.

What it does not do: It computes nothing itself; every number is read from the same files the other pages use. The Moon Base finding is a statement about what is in PSI, not a safety judgement.
