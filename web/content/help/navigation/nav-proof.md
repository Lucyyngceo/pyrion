What it is: The Proof entry of the More menu.

What it shows: The page of checks behind PYRION: the judge battery, located values, validator findings, tests and sealed predictions.

How to use it: Open More, then Proof.

Example: Read the latest battery result and its date.

What it does not do: It reads files of the repository; none of its numbers is typed in.
