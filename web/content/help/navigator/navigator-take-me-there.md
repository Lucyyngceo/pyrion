The right column, "Where the evidence is", lists the investigations that already apply and, for the others, the smallest change to your condition that would make each one comparable.

"Take me there" moves your condition to that point: the marker travels, the controls follow, and the verdict updates. A different regime is never a small step, so those routes say "Switch to ..." instead. "No route found" lists investigations that cannot be reached by a change, with the reason.

Applying a route leaves the preset: "Your condition" lights.
