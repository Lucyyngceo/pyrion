What it is: A small outlined label on a route line.

What it shows: "just outside", "outside", "far outside", "untested combination", "Insufficient" or "Other regime".

How to use it: Hover it for the full phrase.

Example: Read it before clicking Take me there.

What it does not do: A distance, not a pass: even "just outside" is an extrapolation.
