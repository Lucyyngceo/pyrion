What it is: The full-width TARS chat.

What it shows: A conversation list, mode switch, skills, the log and the input.

How to use it: Ask a question; read the three layers.

Example: Ask about oxygen partial pressure at 34 % and 8.2 psia.

What it does not do: TARS answers verdicts only through the engine.
