What it is: One sentence with the share of the band inside the region spanned by PSI test points.

What it shows: The covered share of grid cells, and the ERL badge.

How to use it: Read it; toggle a layer to see the planned share.

Example: 0 % of the band is covered today.

What it does not do: Coverage is the share of grid cells where an investigation is comparable; it is not safety.
