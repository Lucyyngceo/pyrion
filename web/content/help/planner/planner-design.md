What it is: A section where you try hypothetical test points.

What it shows: The coverage with your points, recomputed by the engine.

How to use it: Click the plot, pick a fuel phase, read the result.

Example: Add a point at 34 % and 10.2 psia.

What it does not do: Always hypothetical, not a measurement, never saved.
