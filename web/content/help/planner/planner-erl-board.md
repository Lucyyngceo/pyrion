What it is: A table of habitats by fuel phase with their evidence level.

What it shows: ERL level and name per cell; hover for the reason.

How to use it: Hover a cell.

Example: ISS cabin air with solid fuel.

What it does not do: The spacesuit row is outside PSI and not evaluated.
