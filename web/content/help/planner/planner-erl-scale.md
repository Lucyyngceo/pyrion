What it is: The list of ERL levels.

What it shows: What each level means.

How to use it: Read it.

Example: ERL 4 is labelled "inside the tested-point envelope" on the page. It means the condition lies inside the region spanned by tested points, within the stated tolerance; it does not establish a test at that exact condition.

What it does not do: It is not a safety scale.
