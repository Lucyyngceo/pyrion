What it is: The fewest test points whose outline covers the band under PYRION's geometric convention. It is not a test programme: it holds for one material, flow velocity and sample thickness at a time, and repeats and flammability thresholds are not included.

What it shows: The corners of the band, which FM2 already contributes and which are missing.

How to use it: Read the table.

Example: The 10.2 psia corners are missing.

What it does not do: It says nothing about how many repeats a test needs.
