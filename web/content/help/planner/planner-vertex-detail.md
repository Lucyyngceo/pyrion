What it is: A line under the plot that explains the vertex you point at.

What it shows: Its value as written and its source quote, or the note that PYRION chose the corner.

How to use it: Hover or focus a vertex.

Example: Hover the 8.2 psia / 34 % corner.

What it does not do: Closing corners have no source; the page says so.
