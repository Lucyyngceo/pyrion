What it is: The two-line text of a route.

What it shows: What to change: for example "Move oxygen from 21 % to 21.5 %, the nearest condition tested in PSI-26".

How to use it: Hover for the full text.

Example: Read the change before applying it.

What it does not do: It is the smallest change by the engine's rule, not the best experiment.
