The strip at the top of the Envelope page has four tiles. Click one to load it into the dashboard.

ISS cabin air, NASA's Moon Base (as stated in the Flame in Freefall challenge) and the NASA exploration atmosphere (from an NESC report) are conditions NASA has written down; hover a tile for its source. "Your condition" shows the current controls.

Every number on a tile is computed live by the engine on the real records. Changing any control leaves the preset and lights "Your condition".
