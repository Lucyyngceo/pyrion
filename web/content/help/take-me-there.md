What it is: A small button at the right of a route.

What it shows: It moves your condition to the point that makes that investigation applicable.

How to use it: Click it: the marker travels and the verdict updates.

Example: Click it on PSI-26's route.

What it does not do: A different regime is never a small step: those buttons say "Switch to ...". Applying a route leaves the preset.
