What it is: The 🧠 layer.

What it shows: TARS's own reasoning, labelled "not verified against a source".

How to use it: Treat it as an explanation, not a source.

Example: "Why a flame is round in microgravity".

What it does not do: Numbers without a source are replaced by a removed-number marker.
