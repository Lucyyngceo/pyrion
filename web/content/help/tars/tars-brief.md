What it is: A panel that turns this conversation into a document.

What it shows: A preview of the brief with a bibliography and the engine fingerprints.

How to use it: Press Build the brief, then download it or print it.

Example: Brief a conversation about the ISS cabin air.

What it does not do: Only checked statements are included; the model is not called.
