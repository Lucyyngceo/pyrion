What it is: A NASA quote in an answer. It is focusable and reacts to hover.

What it shows: On hover or focus, the real text of the cited page from the NASA library, with the quoted sentence highlighted, and the document link.

How to use it: Hover it, or tab to it, to read the quote in context. Press Esc to close the preview.

Example: Hover a quote about FM2 to see the whole page it comes from.

What it does not do: It does not re-check the quote (code already did that before the answer was shown), and it shows one page, not the whole document.
