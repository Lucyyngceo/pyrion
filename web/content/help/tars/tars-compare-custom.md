What it is: Three boxes: name, oxygen in percent, pressure with unit.

What it shows: Nothing until you compare.

How to use it: Write the oxygen as a number and the pressure with its unit, such as 8.2 psia.

Example: 34 and 8.2 psia.

What it does not do: It refuses a unit it cannot read instead of guessing.
