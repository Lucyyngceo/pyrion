What it is: A button that draws the compared points on the Envelope map.

What it shows: Numbered diamonds on the map, one per condition.

How to use it: Press it; the Envelope page opens if you are elsewhere.

Example: Numbers on the map match the numbers in the table.

What it does not do: It draws points only; verdicts stay on the cards.
