What it is: The comparison button, labelled Compare followed by the number of selected conditions.

What it shows: Nothing until pressed.

How to use it: Press it with at least two conditions chosen.

Example: Pick two scenarios and press it.

What it does not do: It is computed by code and does not use a model.
