What it is: Three buttons.

What it shows: Easy: plain wording, steps folded. Advanced: figures, receipts, steps open. Auto: Advanced for a condition, Easy for a how-to question.

How to use it: Click one.

Example: Switch to Easy to fold the steps.

What it does not do: The facts are identical in all modes.
