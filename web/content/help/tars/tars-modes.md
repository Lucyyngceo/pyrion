title: Easy, Advanced and Auto
chapter: tars
order: 3
---
**Easy** uses plain wording and folds the steps and the receipts. **Advanced** shows the figures, the receipts, the model that answered and the steps open. **Auto** chooses Advanced for a question about a condition and Easy for a how-to question.

The facts are the same in every mode. Only the wording and the amount of detail change.
