What it is: Up to three small buttons under an answer.

What it shows: Follow-ups that fit the answer, for example what would close the gap, a comparison with ISS air, or showing the condition on the envelope.

How to use it: Click one to ask it, or to load the condition.

Example: After an answer about 34 % oxygen at 8.2 psia, click "Compare with ISS air".

What it does not do: The suggestions are fixed templates chosen by code from what the answer used; they are not written by the model and they are not predictions.
