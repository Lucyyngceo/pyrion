What it is: A button under an answer that has a condition.

What it shows: Copies a link that holds the question and the condition.

How to use it: Click it and paste the link to someone.

Example: Send the link to a teammate.

What it does not do: Nothing is stored on a server; whoever opens it sees the engine part reproduced, not the model's words.
