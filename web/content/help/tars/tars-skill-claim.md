What it is: A skill button in the TARS panel.

What it shows: Opens the Check a claim panel.

How to use it: Click it, paste a text, press Check the claims.

Example: Paste a paragraph that says a flame is safe at a condition.

What it does not do: It does not decide anything by itself: classes come from code.
