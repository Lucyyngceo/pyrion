**Map the unknown** takes the condition on the Envelope page and answers in three parts: what is known (with receipts), what is unknown and why, and what would close the gap.

**What would close this gap** shows the engine's plan of experiments for the same condition.

Both need a current condition. If there is none, TARS asks you for one.
