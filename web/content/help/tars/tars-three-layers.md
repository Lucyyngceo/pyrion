**🔒 Engine.** Facts the engine or a calculator computed: verdicts, counts, partial pressures. Any number here must come from a tool result.

**📄 NASA said it.** A paraphrase with an exact quote, the document title, the page and a link. The quote is checked against the cited page by code; if it is not found there, the whole block is withheld.

**🧠 TARS expert knowledge.** TARS's own explanation of the physics. It is labelled "not verified against a source". A number in it that is not from a tool or a verified quote is replaced by "[number removed: not from a source]".

When a block is withheld or a number removed, a small line says so.
