TARS SI stands for Traceable Applicability Reasoning System. It is the chat of PYRION: a bubble at the bottom right of every page, and a full page under the **TARS SI** tab.

You can ask it about a condition, about NASA documents on combustion and spacecraft fire safety, or about this site. It works like a colleague who knows the subject and shows its sources.

What makes it different from a general chat is that its answer is split into layers, and code checks each layer before you read it.
