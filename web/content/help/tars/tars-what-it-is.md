TARS SI stands for Traceable Applicability Reasoning System. It is the chat of PYRION: a bubble at the bottom right of every page, and a full page reached through **Ask TARS** in the top bar.

You can ask it about a condition, about NASA documents on combustion and spacecraft fire safety, or about this site. It works like a colleague who knows the subject and shows its sources.

Answers are split into labelled layers. Code checks quoted text and numbers against available sources and tool results. These checks do not verify the scientific correctness of the own-reasoning layer.
