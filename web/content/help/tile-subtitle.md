What it is: The small line under a tile title.

What it shows: Where the condition comes from, for example "as stated in the Flame in Freefall challenge".

How to use it: Read it before trusting a tile.

Example: The exploration tile cites its NTRS document.

What it does not do: It is not a quote of the full source; hover the tile for that.
