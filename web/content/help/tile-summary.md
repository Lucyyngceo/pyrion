What it is: The bold line on a tile.

What it shows: How many investigations apply, split by fuel phase, and how many need extrapolation; or, when none apply, the headline of the chart.

How to use it: Read it to see how much evidence PSI holds for that scenario.

Example: "4 applicable (3 gas fuel, 1 liquid fuel)".

What it does not do: Applicable means a PSI test covered the oxygen and pressure in the same regime; it does not mean the condition is safe.
