What it is: A button inside a card that is insufficient or not comparable.

What it shows: The change that would resolve it: leave an axis open, or switch regime.

How to use it: Click it to apply the change.

Example: Leave diluent open.

What it does not do: A regime switch is shown as another regime, never as a small step.
