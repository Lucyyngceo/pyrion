What it is: The card of one investigation.

What it shows: Its verdict badge, a one-sentence result, title, DOI, the reasons and the receipt.

How to use it: Click it, or open it from the chart.

Example: Read the card for PSI-69.

What it does not do: It states what was extracted; it does not re-judge the experiment.
