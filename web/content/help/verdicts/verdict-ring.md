What it is: The circular counter.

What it shows: The number of investigations with extracted records, split by verdict.

How to use it: Hover a row for the split by extrapolation level.

Example: Read how many are applicable.

What it does not do: Investigations with no extracted values yet are not in the ring; the caption under it says how many.
