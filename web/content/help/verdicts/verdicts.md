Each investigation gets one verdict for your condition.

Applicable: the investigation tested the same regime, and your oxygen and pressure fall inside what it measured.

Extrapolation: same regime, but your condition lies outside the measured range. Any value would have to be extended beyond the measurements.

Insufficient evidence: the extracted records do not state enough to compare. PYRION does not guess.

Not comparable (different regime): gravity, fuel phase, diluent or flame type differ. This is a barrier, not a worse match, so no distance is computed across it.
