What it is: The button and the link above a chapter.

What it shows: The print dialog of your browser, where you can choose Save as PDF; the link opens the whole manual on one page.

How to use it: Click it, or open the whole manual and print.

Example: Open the whole manual and save it as PDF.

What it does not do: It prints the text on screen; it does not add images.
