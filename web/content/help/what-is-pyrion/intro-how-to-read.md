The manual has twelve chapters in a fixed order. Chapter 2 walks through the Envelope page, the home page, element by element. Chapters 3 to 6 explain the right-hand column, the verdicts, the plan of experiments and the Add a source dialog. Chapter 7 is TARS SI, the chat. Chapter 8 is the Bridge. Chapters 9 and 10 explain the data and the method. Chapter 11 is a glossary and chapter 12 lists what PYRION does not claim.

Every topic about something on screen has a **Show me** button. It takes you to the page and outlines the element. In the ? menu, **Help mode** does the reverse: turn it on, click any element, and its topic opens.

If a sentence in this manual describes something that is not built, it says so. Nothing here describes an unbuilt feature as working.
