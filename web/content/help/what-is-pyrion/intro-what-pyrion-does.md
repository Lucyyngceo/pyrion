NASA has run many combustion experiments in microgravity and published their data in PSI, the Physical Sciences Informatics archive. A question that matters for spacecraft and for a Moon base is: **does what NASA measured apply to the condition I care about?** A condition here means three things: how much oxygen is in the air, how high the pressure is, and what gravity there is.

PYRION answers that question with a deterministic engine. For each investigation it asks whether your oxygen and pressure fall inside what that investigation tested, in the same physical regime. It then gives one of four verdicts and shows exactly which values it used, with the file and location they were read from.

Where PSI has no test, PYRION says so. It does not fill the gap with a guess, and it never says a condition is safe.
